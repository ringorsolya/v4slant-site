Numerous studies have shown that mass media channels influence public and individual opinions on social problems, the economic situation, politics, and current events. The online press also plays a crucial role in this, as it can influence readers' opinions not only by selecting the content to be published but also by contextualizing the news and articles and how they are presented.

## The influence of the media

The media create a media representation of events by presenting different content, but this "reality" is to some extent constructed ([Reifová, 2004](https://is.muni.cz/th/fh3tn/DP_UHALOVA_oerev.pdf); [Uhaľová, 2017](https://is.muni.cz/th/fh3tn/DP_UHALOVA_oerev.pdf)) based on criteria and editorial values ([Kunczik, 1995, in Uhaľová, 2017](https://is.muni.cz/th/fh3tn/DP_UHALOVA_oerev.pdf)). The editorial staff determines which events to cover, to what extent, which personnel to show, which to give less or more attention to, and what message to transmit to the reader, according to its values and goals.

According to Haselmayer and his associates ([2017](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5679709/)), the selection of news to be displayed, the so-called "gatekeeper activity", is determined by both the supply and the demand side. On the supply side, there are individuals, events and their messages that are close to the editorial viewpoint and ideology, while on the demand side stand the needs and capacity of the readership. Another important factor is the news value, which can be determined by the presence or absence of certain news factors ([Staab, 1990, in Haselmayer, Wagner & Meyer, 2017](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5679709/)), including "the role of power elites, conflict, surprise, relevance and continuity of the topic on the media agenda" ([Haselmayer, Wagner & Meyer, 2017](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5679709/)). Wardle ([2020](https://firstdraftnews.org/long-form-article/understanding-information-disorder/)) distinguishes, among others, the following types of content: clickbait content, misleading content, real content reframed with false context, deceptive content, manipulated content and fabricated content.

## The topic of the research

Our research investigated the critical situation of the Slovak health care system in the online press in Slovakia, on the six most visited Slovak and two other Hungarian online news portals. We used qualitative and quantitative methods to investigate how the articles published by each news portal portray the critical situation in the health sector and how they influence the reader.

Two critical situations are addressed in the research:

1. the announcement of doctors' readiness to strike (2022);
2. the mass resignation of doctors and the possible collapse of the health care system (2022).

The doctors' strike preparedness was reported in the daily press on the same day as the announcement, 5 April 2022. On the same day, the Slovak Ministry of Health reacted to the doctors' union's announcement, and several daily press reports also reported on this. The government had until the end of November 2022 to take the necessary steps so that the doctors would withdraw their resignation. Before the deadline, the most widely read daily newspapers covered the situation, with articles on the doctors' mass resignations and the actions of the Ministry of Health and the government concerning the case.

## Results

The target audiences of the different news portals differ, and the text and style of the articles are tailored to the needs of the reader. All the news portals use the terms "crisis" and "threat"/"compromise" at least once in the title, and several times in the texts. The online press mentioned politicians in similar proportions, typically in reports taken from the Slovak News Agency. The online news portals included in the study take a significant number of articles from news agencies.

The results show that the researched online press, to varying degrees but equally typically, often make a text taken from a news agency more interesting by using different, more or less eye-catching titles and subheadings and by highlighting paragraphs and sections of text to make it different from similar articles in other outlets. Overall, the main influencing factor in the online press studied is how the news on the subject under investigation is presented.
