We collected around five million articles from Czech, Hungarian, Polish and Slovak news portals and applied text mining and deep-learning language models to show how online news frames crises such as the pandemic and the war in Ukraine.

The corpus, code and emotion models are openly available, and an interactive dashboard presents the analysis of 2.9 million articles from 35 outlets.

  • Visegrad Fund project 22310057
  • October 2023 – June 2025
  • Led by the ELTE Centre for Social Sciences, Budapest

  • Emotion modelsFine-tuned XLM-RoBERTa models for Czech, Hungarian, Polish and Slovak.
