Visegrad Fund project 22310057

{{ site.long_title }}

Our project applies text mining and natural language processing methods, including novel approaches based on language modelling and deep learning, to identify slant in online news and so promote resilience against biased reporting.

We analysed around five million articles from Czech, Hungarian, Polish and Slovak news portals published between 2018 and 2024.

1 October 2023 – 30 June 2025 · Led by the HUN-REN Centre for Social Sciences, Budapest
