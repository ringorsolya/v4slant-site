The V4 Media Slant project co-organised the 2nd Budapest Methods Workshop, held on 21–22 November 2024 at the Centre for Social Sciences in Budapest. Project partners presented key findings of the project.

Five panels addressed media analysis methodologies, multilingual applications of large language models, emotion detection in political discourse, comparative political communication, and the ethical and legal aspects of AI. Michal Parízek (Charles University) gave the keynote lecture *Tracing International Conflict Narratives with LLMs*, and a matchmaking session connected early-career scholars.
