The Slovak popular science portal *Veda na dosah* published a feature titled *Skresľujú médiá informácie v digitálnej dobe?* [Do the media distort information in the digital age?]. The article presents the work of our partner, the Kempelen Institute of Intelligent Technologies, on identifying and understanding the often unconscious biases in media texts, carried out jointly by researchers of the Visegrad Group countries.
