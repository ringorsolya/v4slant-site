Orsolya Ring, Martina Katalin Szabó, Csenge Guba, Bendegúz Váradi and István Üveges published *Approaches to sentiment analysis of Hungarian political news at the sentence level* in *Language Resources and Evaluation* (58(4), 1233–1261).

The study introduces a sentiment and emotion annotation framework, a manually annotated dataset of 5,700 political news sentences, and a new Hungarian sentiment dictionary built with word embeddings. It compares dictionary-based, machine-learning and fine-tuned Hungarian BERT approaches, and tests how pre-processing affects performance on a morphologically rich language.
