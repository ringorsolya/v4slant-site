title: The project starts
date: 2023-10-01
type: Milestone
summary: Work begins on selecting news portals and building a comparable corpus for Czechia, Hungary, Poland and Slovakia.
---
The V4 Media Slant project started on 1 October 2023. In the first phase, the partners selected the online news portals to be analysed in each country, balanced by political orientation and ownership, and began building a corpus with a shared metadata structure.
