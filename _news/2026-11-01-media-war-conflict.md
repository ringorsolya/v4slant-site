Orsolya Ring and Ákos Holányi published *Shifting priorities? Coverage of the Russia-Ukraine war in Central European online news media (2022–2026)* in *Media, War & Conflict*.

The article analyses the bureaucratic, defence, economic and humanitarian dimensions of Russia–Ukraine war coverage between January 2022 and February 2026 in eight Central European news outlets. It finds no evidence of war fatigue in the thematic salience of war-related coverage: the war instead became a routine issue in Central European media. National contexts and political leanings shaped the temporal dynamics of the four dimensions, but not their overall salience.
