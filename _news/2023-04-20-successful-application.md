---
title: Successful application to the International Visegrad Fund
date: 2023-04-20
type: Milestone
summary: The Visegrad Fund approved the grant proposal "Identifying News Slant in Crisis Communication Using Artificial Intelligence".
---
The Visegrad Fund approved the funding of the grant proposal *Identifying News Slant in Crisis Communication Using Artificial Intelligence* of the Centre for Social Sciences. Orsolya Ring, research fellow at the Institute for Political Science and senior researcher at poltextLAB, leads the one-and-a-half-year research programme.

With partners from the Czech Republic, Poland and Slovakia, the project investigates the bias associated with different crises in online media using artificial intelligence.
