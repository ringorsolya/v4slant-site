László Kiss's article *A médiatorzítástól a párhuzamos valóságokig* [From media bias to parallel realities] appeared in the summer 2024 issue of *Médiakutató*.

The study compares how origo.hu and telex.hu, two online portals with opposing political alignments, covered the teachers' protests and the state of public education in Hungary. It shows that beyond different narratives, the two portals strongly differ in news selection and framing. A blog post based on the study, [Readings of a protest]({{ '/blog/readings-of-a-protest/' | relative_url }}), discusses one event in detail.
