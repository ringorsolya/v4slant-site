The Hungarian project team presented the project's key findings to a non-specialist, Hungarian-speaking audience in the *Határtalan Tudás* (Boundless Knowledge) public lecture series of Central European University, in the session *Mit adhat az AI a tudománynak?* [What can AI offer to science?].
