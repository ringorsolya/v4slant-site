At the 6th Annual COMPTEXT Conference, Orsolya Ring presented the paper *Uncovering disinformation: analysing the thematic patterns and emotional content of Hungarian disinformation portals during the COVID-19 and the Ukrainian war crises* (co-authored with László Kiss).

The study asks whether AI-based approaches can identify features of fake news that are typical across crises, so that the method can be applied to detect disinformation in future crisis situations.
