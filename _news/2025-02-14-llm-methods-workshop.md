On 14 February 2025, the project held the international *Methods Workshop on Large Language Models and Generative AI: Advancing Policy Frame and Media Narrative Analysis* at Charles University in Prague, co-organised by Charles University and the ELTE Centre for Social Sciences.

The workshop focused on methodological advances in using large language models for media and policy analysis, with presentations on multilingual sentiment analysis, synthetic data generation and LLM-based content analysis. Project members presented the corpus, the emotion models and the interactive dashboard, while external speakers offered comparative perspectives on generative AI in media research.

The event brought together 52 participants from all four Visegrad countries as well as Germany and Italy, and launched an annual conference series of the consortium.
