On 14 February 2025, the project held the international *Methods Workshop on Large Language Models and Generative AI: Advancing Policy Frame and Media Narrative Analysis*.

The workshop brought together 52 participants from all four Visegrad countries as well as Germany and Italy. Project members presented corpus building, AI-based emotion modelling and the interactive dashboard, while external speakers offered comparative perspectives on generative AI in media analysis. The workshop launched an annual conference series of the consortium.
