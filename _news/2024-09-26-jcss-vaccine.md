Miklós Sebők, Orsolya Ring, Márk György Kis, Martin Balázs Bánóczy and Ágnes Dinnyés published *The geopolitics of vaccine media representation in Orbán's Hungary – an AI-supported sentiment analysis* in the *Journal of Computational Social Science* (7, 2897–2920).

The article examines how domestic politics and geopolitics, not only the severity of the pandemic, shaped Hungarian media coverage of COVID-19 vaccines from Western, Russian and Chinese manufacturers.
