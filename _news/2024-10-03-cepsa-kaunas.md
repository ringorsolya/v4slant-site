title: The depiction of the war in Ukraine at the CEPSA conference
date: 2024-10-03
type: Conference
place: Kaunas
summary: Ákos Holányi presented first results on Czech and Hungarian media coverage of the Russia–Ukraine war.
---
On the first day of the 28th Annual Conference of the Central European Political Science Association (CEPSA) at Vytautas Magnus University in Kaunas, Ákos Holányi took part in the panel *The Russian war in Ukraine* with research from the V4 Media Slant project.

His presentation showed preliminary results on how the topics and emotional content of Czech and Hungarian media coverage of the war changed in the first two years of the invasion.
