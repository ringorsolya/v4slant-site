István Üveges and Orsolya Ring published *Evaluating the Impact of Synthetic Data on Emotion Classification: A Linguistic and Structural Analysis* in *Information* (16(4), 330).

Class imbalance is a key challenge in emotion classification, as dominant emotion categories overshadow less frequent ones. The article evaluates synthetic data generated by large language models as a way to augment minority classes. The paper is the reference publication for the project's [emotion models on Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/visegradmedia-emotion).
